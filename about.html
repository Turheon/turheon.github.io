About Turheon

Intelligence built with clarity, robustness, and long-term systems thinking.

Turheon bridges rigorous AI research with real-world decision environments. We focus on optimisation, preference modelling, and the responsible deployment of intelligent systems at scale.

Our philosophy is simple: intelligence must be transparent, accountable, and profoundly aligned with human and ecological systems.
